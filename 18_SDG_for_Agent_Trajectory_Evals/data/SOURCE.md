## What we changed

This is **not** the full official text. To keep it a focused, affordable RAG
corpus we converted the EUR-Lex HTML to Markdown and kept only:

- the **enacting terms** — Chapters I–XIII, i.e. all 113 Articles, and
- **Annex III** (the list of high-risk AI systems).

We dropped the 180 recitals, the citations/masthead, footnote markers, and the
other twelve annexes. Article headings are formatted as `## Article N — Title`;
lettered/numbered points are flattened to single lines.

For the authoritative, complete, legally binding text, always refer to EUR-Lex.
